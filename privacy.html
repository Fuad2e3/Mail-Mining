Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information EmailFinder ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use our website-intelligence and lead-mining tool (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored securely by our backend; we do not store plaintext passwords in your browser) when you register.
  • Submitted mining data — the website URLs, domains, and extraction parameters you provide, along with the resulting extracted data (company names, public contact details, social links, page content) generated by the Service on your behalf.
  • Usage & device data — basic technical information such as browser type, theme preference, and feature usage, used to operate and improve the Service.
  • Local storage data — session tokens, theme preference, and cached UI state kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in and don't lose your settings between visits.

How We Use Information

We use the information above to: provide and operate the Service (authenticate you, run mining sessions, track your daily credit usage); maintain and improve reliability and performance; communicate service-related notices (announcements, account status changes); and enforce our Terms of Service and prevent abuse.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Extracted mining data and account information may pass through third-party infrastructure strictly to operate the Service, for example CORS-proxy fallback providers used to fetch publicly available website content, and export libraries (SheetJS, jsPDF) that run entirely in your browser to generate your downloaded files. We do not share your account data with advertisers.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to keep you signed in, remember your theme preference, and cache UI state. You can clear this at any time from your browser settings, which will sign you out.

Data Retention

We retain account and mining-session data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we'll respond as soon as reasonably possible.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
