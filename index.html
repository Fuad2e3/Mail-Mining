FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a real email verification service?
No — EmailFinder is currently a frontend-only demo. All verification results are simulated using heuristic scoring. No real SMTP, DNS, or MX lookups are performed. This is clearly indicated throughout the interface.
Does my CSV data leave my device?
Absolutely not. All CSV processing happens locally in your browser using the FileReader API. No data is transmitted to any server.
What email patterns are generated?
EmailFinder generates 12 common professional patterns including: first.last, firstlast, f.last, flast, first, last, last.first, first_last, first.l, firstl, fl, and more. All patterns are normalized for special characters.
Where is my search history stored?
Search history is saved in your browser's localStorage only. It never leaves your device. You can clear it anytime from the History page or Settings.
Will there be a real backend?
This frontend demonstrates the full UX of a future EmailFinder SaaS platform. A real backend with live DNS/MX/SMTP verification, user accounts, and credit billing is planned for a future release.
